About the brotherhood

What brings us together

Being part of a brotherhood means being there for one another. It means showing respect, taking responsibility, and making time to help.

Our chapter exists to build closer relationships among members, encourage an intellectual environment that connects students with each other and with faculty, and to develop good moral character, personal discipline, and civic consciousness. We also share in the responsibility of contributing to the welfare of the wider community.

Brotherhood

Community service

Personal growth

What our name and seal represent

Zeta Psi Omicron — a name that stands for "Zeal for Organization." Every element of our seal carries meaning:

  • Sky blue — universal brotherhood
  • Golden yellow — sincerity
  • White — purity
  • Torch — enlightenment, and the responsibilities that come with membership
  • Book — the value the organization places on education and knowledge
  • Golden shower tree — the moral guidance the organization offers its members
  • Rings — honoring the chapter's founding members
  • Cross — unity that reaches every direction, near and far
Our history

Our History

1965

Year of founding, as inscribed on the official seal.

Zeta Psi Omicron Fraternity and Psi Zeta Omicron Sorority were founded together in 1965 at the University of the Philippines Los Baños. Members of the organization are known as “Zepsomians.”

Published Philippine fraternity directories record the founding date as January 29, 1965.

Growth of the organization

Based on the fraternity's own constitution and by-laws, ratified at national conventions over the years.

2006

Seal standardized

The 1st National Convention approved an official format for the organization's seal.

2008

Anti-hazing compliance

The 2nd National Convention formally adopted compliance with the Philippine Anti-Hazing Law.

2010

Locality-based chapters

The 3rd National Convention allowed chapters to organize by locality rather than by school alone, uniting students and alumni in the same area.

2012

National leadership

The 4th National Convention created a national governing body, with a National President and Vice Presidents representing Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao.

Beyond one chapter

National organization

Zeta Psi Omicron began as a single chapter at UP Los Baños in 1965, but has since grown into a nationally organized fraternity and sorority with chapters across the Philippines — including our own chapter here in Malaybalay City, Bukidnon.

Since 2012, the organization has been guided by a national governing body, alongside the leadership of each local chapter.

  • National President
  • Vice President for Luzon
  • Vice President for Visayas
  • Vice President for Mindanao
  • National Secretary
  • National Treasurer
  • National Auditor
Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Does applying mean I am already a member?

No. The form introduces you to the chapter. Orientation and evaluation are part of the membership process.

What are the fees and time commitments?

These details are not published here yet. Ask the chapter about costs, meeting schedules, and activity expectations before committing.

When is the next orientation?

The next orientation is on October 17, 2026, from 6:00 PM onwards. Call time is 5:30 PM (Philippine Time). Submit your application for follow-up details from the chapter.

What does "Zeta Psi Omicron" mean?

The name translates to "Zeal for Organization," reflecting the fraternity's founding commitment to purposeful, organized brotherhood.

What is the fraternity's motto?

"Unity Creates an Organization" — the belief that a strong union comes from each member's contribution.

What's the difference between the fraternity and the sorority?

Zeta Psi Omicron (fraternity) and Psi Zeta Omicron (sorority) are companion organizations founded together in 1965, sharing the same values, motto, and governing constitution.

Does the organization allow hazing?

No. The fraternity formally adopted compliance with the Philippine Anti-Hazing Law (RA 8049) in 2008, and community service is used as part of the membership process.

How do I become a member?

Prospective members must be enrolled, bona fide students with at least two semesters of standing and satisfactory academic performance. See the Membership section above for details.
