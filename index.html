About the brotherhood

What brings us together

Being part of a brotherhood means being there for one another. It means showing respect, taking responsibility, and making time to help.

Brotherhood

Look out for one another and build friendships based on trust and respect.

Leadership

Take responsibility for your actions and set a good example for others.

Community service

Pay attention to what people need and help where you can.

Personal growth

Learn from one another, accept feedback, and keep improving.

What our name and seal represent

Zeta Psi Omicron — a name that stands for "Zeal for Organization." Every element of our seal carries meaning:

  • Sky blue — universal brotherhood
  • Golden yellow — sincerity
  • White — purity
  • Torch — enlightenment, and the responsibilities that come with membership
  • Book — the value the organization places on education and knowledge
  • Golden shower tree — the moral guidance the organization offers its members
  • Rings — honoring the chapter's founding members
  • Cross — unity that reaches every direction, near and far
Our history

The story so far

1965

The year displayed on the fraternity emblem.

Older public fraternity directories list Zeta Psi Omicron as having been founded on January 29, 1965, at the University of the Philippines Los Baños. They also use the name “Zepsomians” and the description “Group of Student Scholars.”

These details come from secondary directories and remain unconfirmed against official fraternity records. The founders’ names and a fuller account of the early years still require verification. The directories may share the same underlying source.

Join us

Membership

Zeta Psi Omicron welcomes students who share our commitment to brotherhood, leadership, and service. Every prospective member goes through an orientation and evaluation period before being welcomed as a full, bonafide member of the chapter.

Learn about orientation

  • Currently enrolled, bonafide student of the institution
  • At least two semesters of standing in the college
  • Maintains satisfactory academic standing
  • Completes the chapter's orientation and evaluation process
Chapter leadership

Officers of the chapter

President

Presides over chapter meetings and represents the organization.

Vice President

Supports the President and steps in whenever needed.

Secretary

Maintains chapter records and handles official correspondence.

Treasurer

Oversees chapter finances and prepares financial reports.

Auditor

Reviews financial records for accuracy.

Business Manager

Handles the chapter's fundraising and business activities.

Public Relations Officer

Manages chapter communications, publicity, and events coverage.

Each chapter is also guided by an alumni adviser, chosen by the membership to support and mentor the organization.
